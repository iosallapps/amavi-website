Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective: 15 November 2026 · Română

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use AMAVI ("the App"), an iPhone app for tracking your menstrual cycle and wellbeing, provided by Cirjan Darius ("we", "us"). The short version: your cycle and health logs stay on your iPhone. We have no accounts and no servers that receive them. The App shows ads from Google AdMob, which is the only part that sends data off your device, and it never receives your health logs.

1. Information that stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only in the App's storage on your iPhone:

  • Cycle and health logs: period days and flow, symptoms (such as cramps), mood, energy, sleep, discharge, sexual activity, temperature, medication and notes.
  • Profile and settings: your goal, cycle and period length, last period date, birth date, region, units, optional name, tracking preferences and reminder times.

We cannot see, access or recover this information. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is never used for advertising. If your iPhone backups are turned on (iCloud or computer backups), Apple's backup includes the App's data under your Apple account settings.

2. Predictions, reminders and AMAVI AI

Period, fertile window and ovulation predictions are calculated on your iPhone. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone; no server sends them. AMAVI AI answers with Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence) or with answers built into the App, so your questions and journal context are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

3. Apple Health (optional)

If you turn on Profile > Apple Health, the App reads period days, spotting, basal body temperature, ovulation and pregnancy tests and sexual activity that other apps saved in Apple Health, and saves the same kinds of data you log in the App to Apple Health. This happens on your iPhone through Apple's HealthKit; we do not receive it. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising and never shared with the ad SDK. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows a full-screen App Open ad when you open it or return to it. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show and measure ads, the SDK may collect device and usage data, such as your IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system, ad interactions, diagnostic data and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA).

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Profile > Ad privacy choices.
  • Tracking. iOS asks whether the App may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized based on it. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Your health logs are never shared with the ad SDK.

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

We do not process your cycle or health data on any server; it stays under your control on your device. For ads, personalized advertising relies on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Non-personalized ads and ad fraud prevention rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), as described in Google's consent form.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Your logs stay on your iPhone until you delete them. In the App, Profile > Delete all data (called Log out in earlier versions) deletes all your data from the device and, if Apple Health sync is on, the data the App saved to Apple Health. Deleting the App also deletes its data from your iPhone. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, to object, to restrict processing and to withdraw consent. Because we hold none of your logs, you control them directly in the App. For ad data, use Profile > Ad privacy choices or contact Google. You may complain to your data protection authority; in Romania this is ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and asks for your birth date during setup.

9. Health information

AMAVI provides general information and self-tracking. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition and must not be used as contraception. Talk to a doctor about your health.

10. Changes

If we add features that change how data is handled (for example notifications we send), we will update this page before they ship and change the date above.
