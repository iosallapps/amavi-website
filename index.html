Features

Everything for your cycle,
nothing you did not ask for.

Period and fertility

Forecasts for your next period, fertile window and ovulation, calculated on your iPhone. Estimates, not contraception.

Daily log

Flow, cramps, mood, energy, sleep, temperature and notes, in a tap.

Stays on your phone

No account. Your logs stay on your iPhone, and Delete all data removes them.

Apple Health, if you want

Import periods and temperature that other apps saved, and save what you log. Off until you turn it on.

Discreet reminders

Reminders worded so they never mention your cycle, and widgets for Home and Lock Screen.

Amavi AI

General answers about your cycle from Apple's on-device model (English) or written texts (Romanian). Not medical advice.

Privacy

Sensitive data,
kept where you can see it.

Your logs, profile and settings stay on your iPhone. Amavi has no analytics and no crash-reporting service. The only network traffic is one Google AdMob ad, and what you log is never put into an ad request.

Cycle, symptom and pregnancy data are health data. Read the full explanation in the Privacy Policy, including the Consumer health data section. Amavi has no passcode lock of its own, so protect your iPhone with a passcode.

FAQ

Questions,
answered plainly.

Is Amavi on the App Store yet?

Not yet. Amavi is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone (iOS 18 or later).

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server of ours that receives your logs. In the app, Profile, Delete all data removes everything. See the Privacy Policy for details, including Apple Health and backups.

Can I use Amavi to avoid pregnancy?

No. Amavi is not a contraceptive and not a medical device. Predictions of your period, fertile window and ovulation are estimates and can be wrong. Talk to a doctor about contraception.

Does Amavi show ads?

Yes. Amavi is free and shows one full-screen ad from Google AdMob when you open the app or come back to it. What you log is never put into an ad request. You can change your ad choices in Profile, Ad privacy choices (where the consent form applies) and in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Does Amavi work with Apple Health?

Optionally. If you switch on Profile, Apple Health, Amavi reads periods, spotting, basal body temperature, ovulation and pregnancy tests and sexual activity that other apps saved, and saves what you log. It stays on your iPhone and you can turn it off at any time.
