2. What Amavi does

Amavi is a period and cycle tracker for iPhone. You log periods, flow, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep, temperature, ovulation and pregnancy tests and notes. Amavi estimates your next period, fertile window and ovulation on your iPhone, sends discreet reminders, offers Home and Lock Screen widgets, can sync with Apple Health if you allow it, has pregnancy and postpartum modes, and includes Amavi AI, an assistant that gives general information.

5. Important: results and your responsibility

Amavi is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it does not replace a doctor, midwife or pharmacist. If you have symptoms that worry you, or in an emergency, contact a health professional or call 112 (or your local emergency number).

Amavi is not a contraceptive and is not a method of birth control. Predictions of your period, fertile window and ovulation are estimates calculated from what you log and from averages (a calendar method). They can be wrong, because cycles vary, and they must never be used to prevent pregnancy or to decide when sex is safe. They also cannot promise that you will or will not become pregnant. Talk to a doctor about contraception and fertility.

Amavi AI gives general, educational answers and can make mistakes. On iPhones that support it, English answers come from Apple's on-device model; Romanian answers come from texts written into the app. Do not rely on it for a diagnosis, a dose or a decision about treatment. Data that other apps saved in Apple Health is imported as it is; we do not check it.
