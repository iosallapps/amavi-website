How do I add a past period?

Open Calendar, go back to the month, tap the first day of that period and choose "My period started this day". AMAVI fills in your usual period length; you can adjust any day.

Why do my predictions use 28 and 5 days?

Until you log two full cycles, AMAVI uses the cycle and period length you entered. After that it uses the average of your recent cycles.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account. Profile > Log out deletes everything from the device.

How do I change my ad choices?

Profile > Ad privacy choices, and iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
